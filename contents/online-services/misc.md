## Journaling

* [Dreamwidth](https://www.dreamwidth.org/): Dreamwidth is a blogging and social-journaling platform. People post entries in personal journals, follow other users, comment, and take part in group journals called communities. It’s somewhat similar to LiveJournal, with a focus on written posts and discussion.
