# Live Cameras Around the World

## EarthCam

EarthCam’s directory currently spans **dozens of countries and U.S. states**, and it mixes true live-streaming video with controllable/panoramic cameras and other webcam formats. EarthCam also has an advanced search that lets you filter specifically for streaming video. [EarthCam](https://www.earthcam.com/network/index.php?country=United\&page=world\)

Below is a large starter directory emphasizing feeds that are interesting to **watch**, rather than construction cameras and low-interest static views.
