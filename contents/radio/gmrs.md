# GMRS

The [**General Mobile Radio Service**](https://www.fcc.gov/wireless/bureau-divisions/mobility-division/general-mobile-radio-service-gmrs) (GMRS) is a licensed two-way radio service in the United States that operates primarily on frequencies in the 462 and 467 MHz UHF bands. It is commonly used for family communication, road trips, outdoor activities, off-roading, camping, and other situations where short- to medium-range radio communication is useful. GMRS supports handheld, mobile, and base-station radios, as well as repeaters that can substantially extend communication range. Unlike amateur (ham) radio, **there is no examination required to obtain a GMRS license**. An eligible individual age 18 or older can register with the FCC's [**Commission Registration System**](https://www.fcc.gov/licensing-databases/commission-registration-system-fcc) (CORES) to obtain an FCC Registration Number (FRN), then apply for a GMRS license through the FCC's licensing system. The application fee is currently **$35**, and a GMRS license is normally valid for **10 years**.

One useful feature of a GMRS license is that it covers more than just the individual license holder. The FCC allows the licensee's immediate family members—including a spouse, children, parents, grandparents, siblings, aunts, uncles, nieces, nephews, and in-laws—to operate GMRS stations under the same license. After the license is granted, the FCC assigns a **GMRS call sign**, which should be used for station identification when operating on GMRS. The channel and CTCSS tables below provide a quick reference for commonly used GMRS frequencies, repeater pairs, and privacy tones.
